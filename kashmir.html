✨ About Kashmir

Kashmir is famous for its beautiful valleys, lakes, snow-covered mountains and peaceful green meadows. From the Dal Lake in Srinagar to the mountains of Gulmarg and the valleys of Pahalgam, this journey offers a memorable travel experience.

📍 Jammu & Kashmir, India
📅 5 Nights / 6 Days
🏔️ Nature & Adventure
👨‍👩‍👧 Family Friendly

📍 Places You'll Explore

🌊

Srinagar

Enjoy Dal Lake, Shikara rides, houseboats and the famous Mughal Gardens.

❄️

Gulmarg

Experience beautiful meadows, mountain views, snow activities and the famous Gondola ride.

🌲

Pahalgam

Explore green valleys, forests and the beautiful Lidder River.

🏔️

Sonamarg

Enjoy spectacular Himalayan scenery and the beautiful Thajiwas Glacier area.

🌿

A peaceful meadow perfect for nature walks and relaxing away from crowds.

🏞️

Doodhpathri

Discover green meadows, streams and peaceful mountain landscapes.
